案例：银蕨银行虚构
这是一家虚构的新西兰中型银行，每年要处理上万笔个人贷款申请。过去全靠信贷员人工审核：翻看申请人的收入、负债和还款记录，凭经验判断他会不会还不上钱。问题是，每个信贷员的标准都不一样，旺季积压严重，有的客户等了两个星期还没有结果，而且坏账一直降不下来。

于是银行想引入一套人工智能系统：让它学习过去十年里「哪些人最后真的违约了」的历史记录，再给每一笔新申请打一个违约风险分。但银行高层很谨慎：一笔贷款被拒，可能就影响一个家庭买房；万一这套系统带着偏见，专挑某些族裔或低收入的人打高分怎么办？万一申请人问「我为什么被拒」却没人答得上来怎么办？

所以银行请你这位 AI 顾问来设计：用哪一种人工智能、需要哪些数据、怎么和信贷员配合、怎么守住伦理和问责。记住——这套系统不是要取代信贷员，而是要又快又稳，还要经得起监管和投诉的追问。
第 1 题9%该用哪种 AI
识别并解释最适合哪一类 AI 系统，并说明依据。
答案是决策支持型 AI（Decision support AI）。它的任务是根据历史数据预测申请人会不会违约——本质是分类/预测，正是决策支持型 AI 的强项。它不是只按固定规则的自动化 AI，也不是聊天对话的交互型 AI。
英文作答模板（考试用）
The most suitable type is Decision support AI ('optimisation'). The core task is to predict default risk / classify applicants, requiring pattern detection in large historical data. This matches Decision support AI, trained on labelled data to classify new cases. It is not Automation AI (not simple rule-based processing) and not Engagement AI (no conversational element).
第 2 题9%需要哪些数据
识别系统需要的关键数据，并解释为什么必要（要具体）。
最关键的是过去的违约记录——哪些客户最后真的没还上钱，这叫有标签的结果。AI 靠这些标签找出「什么样的人更容易违约」的规律。没有它，模型就没有依据可学，等于盲猜。
英文作答模板（考试用）
The most critical data is the historical default records — the labelled outcome the model is trained on. The algorithm learns to classify new applicants by extracting patterns from these labels; without them, the model has no basis to learn which applicants are likely to default.
第 3 题12%契合战略 + 独特优势
a) AI 如何契合商业战略；b) 相比传统规则工具的独特优势？
a) 银行既要控制坏账风险、又要快速放贷，这套系统能更准地预测风险、减少坏账，正契合战略。
b) 它比人工和死规则强在三点：会从数据学习、能看穿人看不出的规律、会随新数据更新；而且对每个客户用同一套标准，又快又一致。
英文作答模板（考试用）
a) AI fits the strategy because the bank's goal is to manage credit risk while processing loans efficiently; Decision support AI supports this by predicting default risk more accurately, reducing bad debt.

b) Unlike manual review or fixed rules, Decision support AI learns from data and detects complex patterns humans cannot easily see, and updates as new data comes in. Manual review is slower and inconsistent between staff; AI applies the same criteria to everyone.
第 4 题9%数据基础 + 运作
详细解释目标（to-be）流程中的数据基础与运作方式。
建在有标签的历史数据上（过去的申请 + 后来的还款结果）。运作五步：收集 → 清洗准备 → 模型打风险分 → 分数与信贷员判断并排呈现 → 真实结果回流更新。AI 只在「打分」这步帮忙，不替人做决定。
英文作答模板（考试用）
Built on labelled historical data (past applications + known repayment outcomes). Process: ① data collected from bank systems; ② cleaned/prepared, sensitive attributes excluded per regulation; ③ model produces a risk score; ④ score shown to the employee in parallel with their assessment; ⑤ outcomes feed back so the model updates. AI operates at ③–④ as a support layer — scores and flags, does not decide.
第 5 题9%人机协作 + 监督
就人机协作与人类监督给出建议并说明理由。
让 AI 与信贷员平行：各自独立判断，不一致就强制复审，信贷员重新考虑、写下理由再定案，决定权永远在人。理由：放贷是高风险、受监管的决定，责任必须在人；平行能防自动化偏见；AI 当独立交叉校验。
英文作答模板（考试用）
I recommend the AI runs in parallel with the employee rather than ahead. When they disagree in either direction, an additional review is triggered and the employee must reconsider and record a reason before finalizing. The employee always holds the final decision.

Justified because loan approval is a high-stakes, regulated decision, so accountability must rest with a human. Running AI in parallel preserves judgement and avoids automation bias, with the AI as an independent cross-check — improving consistency, not making lending looser.
第 6 题9%改变运用知识的方式
解释 AI 如何改变信贷员运用专业知识完成任务的方式。
以前信贷员靠经验亲自判断。有了 AI，专业不是消失，而是转移：转向解读分数、判断何时该信、处理疑难个案、理解数字背后的情境。要小心去技能化风险——所以「先独立判断再看 AI」的平行设计，正是为了保护专业。
英文作答模板（考试用）
Previously the loan officer relied on their personal experience and judgement to assess each applicant directly. With AI, their expertise shifts rather than disappears — redirected toward interpreting the AI's score and knowing when to trust it, handling borderline cases, and understanding the client's context. This raises a de-skilling risk; the parallel design (judging independently before seeing the AI) preserves their expertise.
第 7 题9%伦理问题 + 对策
描述伦理问题并提出可落地的对策。
三个问题：偏见（历史数据少批穷人/少数族裔 → AI 学并放大，恶性循环）、黑箱（被拒不知原因、无法申诉）、隐私（敏感个人数据）。对策：定期偏见审计+排除受保护属性；要求可解释理由+开放申诉；数据最小化、合规存储。
英文作答模板（考试用）
Concerns: (1) Bias — trains on historical data that under-approved poorer/minority applicants, learning and amplifying it (a self-reinforcing loop). (2) Transparency/black box — rejected applicants aren't told why and can't appeal. (3) Privacy.

Fixes: (1) regular bias audits, monitor/exclude protected attributes; (2) require explainable reasons + an appeal channel; (3) data minimisation and compliant storage.
第 8 题12% (9+3)实施流程
设计逐步实施流程（怎么一步步建到 to-be）。
六阶段：① 数据基础（收集/清洗/打标签）→ ② 开发训练 → ③ 试点（小范围影子运行、不影响真实决定，AI 对比人）→ ④ 评估（准确率/偏见/反馈达标）→ ⑤ 上线（嵌入审批 = to-be）→ ⑥ 监控迭代。深讲挑「试点」：影子模式让错误无代价，上线前先定成功标准。
英文作答模板（考试用）
① Data foundation — collect, clean, label data. ② Develop & train. ③ Pilot — run AI in parallel on a small scale without affecting real decisions, comparing AI vs human. ④ Evaluate — accuracy, bias, staff feedback; proceed only if thresholds met. ⑤ Deploy — embed into the workflow (= the "to-be"). ⑥ Monitor & iterate. Deep-dive (+3%, Pilot): shadow-mode so errors carry no real cost; success criteria agreed before go-live.
第 9 题9%治理机制
提出具体的治理机制。
要具体：① 跨部门 AI 监督委员会（信息官+法务合规+风控+业务）；② 制度化的偏见审计+性能监控；③ 推翻 AI 须留痕写理由；④ 对客户可解释 + 申诉；⑤ 清晰问责链，每个决定有明确负责人；⑥ 数据/模型版本文档与审计记录。每条对应一个伦理问题。
英文作答模板（考试用）
(1) AI oversight committee (CIO + legal/compliance + risk + business). (2) Regular bias audits + performance monitoring. (3) Override logging — staff record a reason when overriding the AI. (4) Explainability + appeal. (5) Clear accountability chain — a defined human responsible for every decision. (6) Documentation & audit trails. Each maps to a Q7 concern.
事件分析12%压轴 · 案例焦点
分析具体事件——相关决策、伦理问题、问责。
三条线：① 哪个决策、AI 起了什么作用（如申请人主要因 AI 高风险分被拒）；② 伦理哪出错（是否偏见？可解释？可申诉？）；③ 谁负责——顺问责链找到定案员工与部署银行，负责的必须是人不是算法；若跳过复审/监督，责任在那里。收尾用治理机制（审计、留痕、申诉）防再发。
英文作答模板（考试用）
The decision: what was decided and AI's role (e.g. the applicant was rejected largely on the AI's risk score).
Ethical concerns: biased against a group? Explainable? Could they appeal?
Accountability: a human, not the algorithm, must be accountable — the employee who finalised, the bank that deployed it; if the override/oversight step was skipped, responsibility lies there.
Close: recommend governance (audit, override logging, appeal) to prevent recurrence.
